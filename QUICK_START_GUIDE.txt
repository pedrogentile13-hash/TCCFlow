QUICK START GUIDE - Enhanced Rich Text Editor
================================================================================

GETTING STARTED WITH THE NEW FEATURES:

1. INSERTING CITATIONS (NEW!)
   ───────────────────────────
   Step 1: Select the text you want to cite
   Step 2: Press Ctrl+Shift+C
   Step 3: A blue superscript number appears (e.g., ¹)
   Step 4: Type the citation reference at the bottom in the footnote
   Step 5: Done! Numbers update automatically

   EXAMPLE:
   Before: "The Earth is round" [select this text]
   Press: Ctrl+Shift+C
   After: "The Earth is round¹"
   
   At bottom:
   ────────────────────────
   ¹ Smith, J. (2020). Modern Geography. Oxford Press.

2. FORMATTING TEXT
   ────────────────
   Ctrl+B        = Bold
   Ctrl+I        = Italic
   Ctrl+U        = Underline
   Ctrl+Shift+M  = Remove all formatting

3. CREATING HEADINGS
   ──────────────────
   Ctrl+Alt+1    = Heading 1 (biggest, main title)
   Ctrl+Alt+2    = Heading 2 (subtitle)
   Ctrl+Alt+3    = Heading 3 (sub-subtitle)
   Ctrl+Alt+4    = Heading 4 (smallest)
   
   These are AUTO-NUMBERED! (1, 1.1, 1.1.1, etc)

4. CREATING LISTS
   ───────────────
   Ctrl+Shift+7  = Numbered list (1, 2, 3...)
   Ctrl+Shift+8  = Bullet points (•, •, •)

5. UNDOING & REDOING
   ──────────────────
   Ctrl+Z        = Undo last action
   Ctrl+Y        = Redo last undone action

6. HELP GUIDE
   ───────────
   Click the ? (question mark) button in the toolbar
   
   Inside you'll find:
   - All keyboard shortcuts
   - Feature explanations
   - Complete citation guide with examples
   - Pro tips and tricks

7. OTHER USEFUL SHORTCUTS
   ───────────────────────
   Ctrl+Shift+K  = Insert hyperlink
   Ctrl+Alt+F    = Insert footnote
   Ctrl+Shift+C  = Insert citation (with auto-numbering)

================================================================================
CITATION SYSTEM - DETAILED EXPLANATION
================================================================================

WHY USE CITATIONS?
Academic papers need citations. This editor makes it EASY.

HOW IT WORKS:

1. SELECT YOUR TEXT
   Choose the sentence/quote you're citing:
   "Climate change affects global weather patterns"

2. PRESS Ctrl+Shift+C
   The text stays there, but a blue number appears:
   "Climate change affects global weather patterns¹"

3. SCROLL TO BOTTOM
   You'll see a new section with:
   ─────────────────────────────
   ¹ [empty field for your citation]

4. TYPE THE CITATION
   "Smith, M. & Johnson, K. (2023). Climate Science Today. Harvard Press."

5. IT SAVES AUTOMATICALLY!
   Everything is saved to your browser's storage.
   Close and reopen? It's still there.

DELETING A CITATION:
- Scroll to footnote area
- Click the × button next to the citation
- All numbers auto-update! (¹, ², ³ becomes ¹, ²)

EDITING A CITATION:
- Click in the footnote text field
- Edit your citation text
- It saves automatically

MULTIPLE CITATIONS:
You can add as many citations as you want:
"First source¹ mentions X, while another study² found Y."

And the footnotes:
─────────────────────────────
¹ First source citation...
² Second source citation...

TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Q: Citation didn't appear after Ctrl+Shift+C?
A: Make sure you selected text BEFORE pressing the shortcut.
   Try again: Select → Ctrl+Shift+C

Q: Numbers aren't auto-updating after deletion?
A: Give it a moment. The app recalculates in the background.
   If it persists, refresh the page.

Q: Can't find the citations at the bottom?
A: Scroll down in the editor. Citations appear at the very bottom.
   They might be below your current view.

Q: Lost my work?
A: Check your browser's localStorage. Ctrl+Alt+1 might be
   typing instead of creating heading? Make sure it's CTRL+ALT (not SHIFT)

Q: Help button not working?
A: Make sure you're clicking the ? icon in the toolbar.
   It's on the right side, after the print button.
